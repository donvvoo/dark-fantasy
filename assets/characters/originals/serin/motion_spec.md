# Serin motion spec (learned from the owner's animation reference sheet)

The reference is a chibi sticker character's sheet. Below are its frame counts and the
way each motion moves. Serin's frames are drawn new; the reference only sets the rhythm.

| motion | frames | what moves |
|---|---|---|
| idle   | 4 | 1-dot breathing bob of head and torso, hair and cape tips sway a dot, feet planted |
| walk   | 8 | feet alternate under the cape, 1-dot bob on passing poses, held item swings a little, head steady |
| run    | 8 | body leans forward 1-2 dots, bigger 2-dot bounce, legs reach wider, hair and cape trail behind |
| jump   | 8 | 2 crouch (squash) -> 3 rising (stretch, arms out) -> apex -> fall -> land squash, back to stand |
| attack | 9 | 4-frame hold with the weapon raised behind the head (anticipation), leap up, slam down and forward with a long red slash streak, 2 follow-through frames, recover |

Rules seen across all motions:
- The facing never changes: three-quarter to the right in every frame.
- The head barely moves; the big head reads as stable, and the body, hair and cape carry the motion.
- Frames are the same canvas, feet on the same baseline except in jump and attack.
- Effects (the slash) are flat bright shapes in one hue with no outline, drawn in front of the character.

For Serin (greatsword on her back): idle, walk and run keep the sword on her back; the
attack draws it over the shoulder in the anticipation frames, slams it down with the slash,
and sheathes it again in the recover frames.
