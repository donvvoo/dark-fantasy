(base_human, outfit_explorer, hair_short), supplied by the project owner on 2026-11-24.
The zip contained no licence file. Before a store release, confirm the pack's terms
(commercial use, credit) on the page it was downloaded from, and record them here.

Everything in `assets/characters/sheets/`, the party sprites (`assets/sprites/knight|archer|cleric|pyro*.png`)
and their frames in `assets/sprites/anim/` are built from these layers by
`tools/charmaker/charmaker.py` and `tools/charmaker/export_party.py`.
